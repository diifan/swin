# Swie Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-09-07
**Provider:** Swink AI Inc. ("we", "us", "Swie")
**Contact:** yiping.zhao@goswi.ai
**Website:** https://goswi.ai

Swie is an on-device golf swing analysis app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

## 1. Summary
- Your **swing videos, photos, audio, and the pose/ball analysis derived from them are processed on your device.** They are stored locally and are **not uploaded to us** unless you explicitly choose to share or export them.
- We use a small **account** (email) so your sessions and Swie‑recorded album follow you across sign‑ins.
- We collect **limited, non‑advertising usage analytics** to understand how features are used and to fix problems.
- We **do not sell your personal data** and we **do not use it for third‑party advertising**.

## 2. Information we collect

**a. Content you create (stays on your device by default)**
Videos, photos, and audio you record or import, and the analysis Swie computes from them (pose keypoints, ball trajectory, swing metrics, reports). This content is stored locally on your device. It leaves your device only if you export it (e.g. save to Photos, share a reel) or if a feature you enable sends it to a service.

**b. Account information**
When you create an account we process your **email address** (and authentication tokens) through our authentication provider, **Supabase**, to sign you in and associate your sessions and Swie album with your account.

**c. Usage analytics**
To improve the app we record **product-usage events** — for example: app opened, a session imported, an editor opened, an auto‑tracer generated, an export completed — along with **event timestamps, a user/device identifier, app version, and basic device model/OS information.** Analytics are stored in our **Supabase** backend. We do **not** attach your swing videos or images to analytics events.

**d. Permissions Swie may request**
- **Camera** — to record your swing (processed on‑device).
- **Microphone** — to capture swing/impact audio with the video.
- **Photo Library** — to import videos to analyze and to save exports you choose.
- **Motion** — to help level the phone on a tripod while framing.
Swie does **not** use speech recognition; that entry exists only because a bundled framework references the API.

## 3. How we use information
- Provide and operate the app (analysis, playback, reels, your album).
- Maintain your account and sync your sessions across sign‑ins.
- Diagnose crashes/bugs and improve features (analytics).
- Comply with law and enforce our Terms.

We do **not** use your content or account to serve advertising, and we do **not** sell personal data.

## 4. Sharing
We share information only with:
- **Service providers** that run our infrastructure — currently **Supabase** (authentication, database/analytics storage). They process data on our behalf under their terms.
- **At your direction** — when you export or share a video/reel.
- **Legal** — if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

## 5. Storage, retention, and security
- On‑device content stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.
- Account and analytics data are retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward, then deleted or anonymized.
- We use industry‑standard measures (e.g. encryption in transit) to protect data, but no method is 100% secure.

## 6. Your choices and rights
- **Permissions:** you can grant or revoke Camera, Microphone, Photos, and Motion access in iOS Settings at any time.
- **Delete your content:** remove sessions/videos in the app or delete the app.
- **Account & data:** to access, correct, or delete your account data, contact us at yiping.zhao@goswi.ai. Depending on where you live (e.g. EEA/UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA), you may have additional rights to access, delete, or port your data and to object to certain processing.

## 7. Children
Swie is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from them.

## 8. International transfers
We may process data in countries other than yours (including where our providers operate). Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

## 9. Changes
We may update this policy; we will change the "Effective date" above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

## 10. Contact
Questions or requests: **yiping.zhao@goswi.ai**.
